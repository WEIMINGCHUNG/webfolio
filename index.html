Interaction Design Researcher

My name is Wei-Ming (David) Chung. I am an interaction design researcher with a Ph.D. in Design from the Department of Design at National Taiwan University of Science and Technology. Over the past 15 years, I have engaged in interdisciplinary research and practice across human–computer interaction (HCI), experience-centered interaction design, and design futuring. My diverse background enables me to facilitate interdisciplinary workshops (e.g., OpenHCI, 2049 AI Summit, and The Future is Now) and collaborate with professionals in AI, IoT, and HCI through large-scale industry–academia projects with the NTU IoX Center, Intel, Cathay Life Insurance, ITRI, Delta, and Taiwan AICoE. My expertise lies in proposing speculative approaches as provocative methods for design thinking and developing designerly tools that foster cross-disciplinary innovation and spark techno-social discussion. By incorporating fictional films, comics, gameplay, audio dramas, sonic design fiction, props, and AI-empowered speculation into early design phases, I invite the interdisciplinary collaborators to playfully co-create future visions surrounding the Internet of Everything (2016–2020, 2025), FinTech (2023), and AI (2024-2025).

Research Interest
